## Editorial changes

The campaign duplicated its entire page; the second copy was removed. Empty resource headings now carry the existing resource links. Unlinked action text now points to working contact/research/legislator pages. “Build Public Steward” became “Build Public Stewardship”; inconsistent capitalization, punctuation, and “Where bands” were corrected. No newsletter signup is invented where no subscription endpoint exists.

Why Flamingos Matter was rewritten to avoid treating flamingo presence as an established quantitative water-quality indicator. Habitat use can inform conservation, while a sighting alone does not prove ecosystem recovery. Wetland services are attributed to EPA. Telemetry results explicitly describe one bird over 22 months. Advocacy benefits are framed as potential outcomes, not guaranteed results. Member affiliations are not presented as institutional endorsements.
